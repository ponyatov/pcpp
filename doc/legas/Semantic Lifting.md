[[Verified Lifting]]. Verified lifting (VL) is a technique that analyzes code from a source PL, lifts
its semantics to a higher level representation, then lowers it into a target PL [6]

[6] Shoaib Kamil, Alvin Cheung, Shachar Itzhaky, and Armando Solar-Lezama. [[Verified Lifting]] of Stencil Computations. In Proceedings of the 37th ACM SIGPLAN Conference on Programming Language Design and Implementation, PLDI ’16, pages 711–726, New York, NY, USA, 2016. ACM.
